Grid & columns

Bootstrap's grid, one-to-one, under sk- names. .sk-grid is .row — a wrapping flex row with negative margins — and .sk-col-{n} is .col-{n}. Gutters are column padding driven by --sk-gutter-x / --sk-gutter-y. Left/right are authored as inline-start/end, so switch Dir → RTL in the deck and offsets and order stay correct.
